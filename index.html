    How to use the Vertuo Pop

    For everyday brewing on the club machine.

    Our pick for milk or ice is an 80 ml double espresso. Use a larger mug to leave room for what you add.

    • For milk · Double Espresso Scuro, Gran Lungo Fortado, Cinnamon Apple Crisp
    • For ice · Double Espresso Active

    1. Check the water

      Fill the tank with fresh drinking water if it’s low, then seat it firmly on the machine.

    2. Pick a capsule and mug

      Choose a fresh Vertuo capsule. Check the brew volume on the lid or sleeve if marked, or use the menu above. Pick a mug with extra room for crema, milk or ice.

    3. Wake the machine

      With the head closed, turn the lever to the lock symbol to wake it. If it’s already locked and off, press the top button once. Wait for a steady light.

    4. Unlock and insert

      Gently turn the lever right to the unlock symbol and lift the head fully. Let any used capsule drop away. Set the fresh capsule in with its dome facing down.

    5. Close, lock and place the mug

      Lower the head. Hold it down gently while turning the lever left to the lock symbol. Center the mug under the spout and adjust the cup support if needed.

    6. Press once to brew

      Press the top coffee button once. The machine reads the capsule and stops automatically. Wait until the flow stops and the light is steady before opening it.

      Cup starting to overflow? Press the coffee button once more to stop brewing. The flow may take a moment to stop.

    7. Eject the used capsule

      Turn the lever right and lift the head so the capsule drops into the used-capsule bin. Close the head and leave the area clean for the next person.

    For first use, rinsing or troubleshooting, see the official Vertuo Pop guide.
